If a selected parameter starts outside its bounds, the fit begins at the nearest bound. Thickness is converted to an integer repeat count N using the mean a and the hkl. Δa is fixed by default, and fitting it does not make it an independently measured strain. The substrate repeat count accepts up to 30,000, but a wide calculation scan can reach the integration limit. The instrumental FWHM is not the full width of the measured peak. The reflection, the wavelength and the material are assumptions you are making.

Thickness is a single film-on-substrate model value from the mean a and the chosen hkl. Δa is the linear change in effective lattice spacing from interface to surface, not an independently measured strain. The peak FWHM is the width of the measured and calculated patterns, film and substrate interference included, and is not the instrumental Gaussian FWHM. Different reflection, wavelength or material assignments give different results. The optimisation does not guarantee a global optimum, so change the starting values, the bounds or the search budget and run it again to check.

Residual = log₁₀(calculated relative intensity) − log₁₀(measured relative intensity). Zero is agreement; +1 means the calculation is ten times higher. Each curve is normalised to its maximum within the fit range.

i

Fitting assumptions

The example file carries no wavelength or reflection information. The default (110), λ = 1.5406 Å and the film material are assumptions for you to confirm. The fit does not decide them for you.

∑

Model scope

A one-dimensional kinematic interference model of a single ABO₃ film on a substrate. Pick the substrate from the list or type its lattice constant in. It is for following trends in repeat counts and lattice constants; dynamical diffraction, the Cu Kα doublet and absolute intensity scaling are not included.
